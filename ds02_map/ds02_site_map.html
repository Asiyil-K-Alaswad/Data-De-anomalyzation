DS02 site locations, fitted to Qatar

The sites are plotted in kilometres from their own centre point, over an outline of Qatar drawn to the same km scale. Rotate, stretch, mirror and move the point cloud until it roughly matches the coastline. The map itself never moves; only the points do. Marker size shows each site's total cell count (2G+4G+5G).

Scroll or pinch to zoom. In move points mode, drag to move the points and Shift+drag to rotate them around the ring marker; in pan view mode, dragging pans the whole view. Hover a site for its details and estimated position.
