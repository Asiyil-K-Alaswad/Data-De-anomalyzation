DS02 sites against OpenCelliD LAC/TAC regions

Qatar is divided into location/tracking areas built from OpenCelliD cells of network 427-02, the operator whose area codes DS02 uses. Each DS02 site is coloured by whether the region it currently sits in carries one of the site's own LAC/TAC codes. Rotate, stretch and move the point cloud to raise the match rate; the regions never move. Marker size shows each site's total cell count.

Scroll or pinch to zoom. In move points mode, drag to move the points and Shift+drag to rotate them around the ring marker; in pan view mode, dragging pans the whole view. Hover a site to outline the region(s) carrying its own codes; pick a code to show only its sites and region.
