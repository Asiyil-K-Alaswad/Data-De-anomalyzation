DS02 site locations, by relative distance

The DS02 coordinates are anonymized, so this map is drawn in kilometres from the network's centre point. Only the distances between sites mean anything; where the sites sit on Earth does not. Marker size shows the site's total cell count (2G+4G+5G).

Kilometres are computed at the anonymized latitude (about 50°S). If the anonymization only shifted the coordinates in degrees, the north–south distances are exact, but the east–west ones scale with the cosine of the true latitude, which isn't known.

Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan. Hover a site for details. The scale bar adjusts as you zoom.
