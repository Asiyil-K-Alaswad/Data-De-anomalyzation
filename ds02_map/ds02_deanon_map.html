DS02 sites at their de-anonymised positions

Each site is moved from its anonymised coordinate to an estimated real position in Qatar by one affine map, fitted so that each LAC/TAC code's sites spread like that code's cells in OpenCelliD. Colours show whether a site now sits in an OpenCelliD region carrying its own code. You can still adjust the points; Reset transform returns them to the fit.

Scroll or pinch to zoom. In move points mode, drag to move the points and Shift+drag to rotate them around the ring marker; in pan view mode, dragging pans the whole view. Hover a site to outline the region(s) carrying its own codes; pick a code to show only its sites and region.
